## Section 5 — Sustainability-linked structures: what would need to be true

**Framing only. Nothing in this section is labelled, offered or committed.** Under CP-011,
no instrument may be described as sustainable, green or transition-aligned before a framework
and a second-party opinion are in place. Gibson has neither today.

**What a credible transition-linked structure would require**

1. **Material KPIs** tied to the issuer's core business — for a midstream operator that means
   operational emissions, methane intensity, water and land reclamation, or safety
   performance. Downstream combustion emissions outside operational control are **not** an
   appropriate KPI under the Bank's framework.
2. **A defensible baseline.** Where an acquisition mechanically changes an intensity metric,
   the baseline is restated so the target cannot be met through portfolio composition alone.
3. **An ambition test** — improvement beyond a business-as-usual trajectory. A target the
   issuer would meet without additional action does not qualify.
4. **Symmetrical, economically meaningful pricing.** A one-basis-point adjustment on a
   facility priced in the low hundreds will not be approved.
5. **A second-party opinion before launch**, and for a private sustainability-linked loan
   above CAD 250m.

**Suggested next step:** a working session between Gibson's sustainability and treasury teams
and the Bank's Sustainable Finance team to test whether the KPI set exists. No mandate implied.
