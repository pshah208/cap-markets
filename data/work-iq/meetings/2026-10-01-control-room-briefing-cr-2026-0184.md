## 1. Purpose

The Control Room convened the coverage team to brief the **existence and effect** of a
restriction on Gibson Energy Inc. (TSX:GEI), without disclosing the underlying matter to
those not wall-crossed.

## 2. Facts stated by the Control Room

- Named individuals were **wall-crossed on 29 September 2026** in connection with a potential
  corporate action involving the client.
- The name was placed on the **restricted list on 1 October 2026**.
- Expected expiry **10 November 2026**. The Control Room stated expressly that an expected
  expiry is an expectation and **not an authorisation to resume on that date** — restrictions
  end only when the Control Room removes them (CP-008).
- The identity of crossed individuals was **not** disclosed to the meeting. Public-side
  attendees were reminded they must not infer or ask.

## 3. Effects for the duration of the restriction

| Function | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Research** | May not initiate, publish, update, change a rating or change a target price on the name. May not participate in marketing for the name. |
| **Sales & Trading** | May not solicit business in the name. Unsolicited client orders may be executed on a strictly agency basis with Control Room notification. Principal risk-taking suspended. |
| **Derivatives** | No new structure connected to the restricted subject matter may be solicited. |
| **Coverage** | Continues. Existing facilities continue to be administered. |
| **Credit Risk** | Continues. Analysis built solely on filed statements and facility documentation is unaffected. |

**What remains permitted.** A pitch based exclusively on public information, provided no
crossed individual contributes non-public content to it and the pitch does not concern the
restricted subject matter. Where there is doubt whether a proposed pitch touches the
restricted matter, **the doubt is resolved by the Control Room, not by the business.**

## 4. The conflict, stated rather than resolved

The Coverage Lead raised — and the Control Room recorded — that the team has a client meeting on
8 October 2026 at which it intends to pitch a financing off the back of a publicly announced
result, while a restriction is live and two of the four intended attendees are crossed.

The Control Room's response was recorded verbatim in substance:

> Escalate the conflict; do not resolve it quietly. Do not narrow the description of the
> agenda to obtain clearance. Do not substitute an uncrossed presenter to make an item
> deliverable. Do not proceed on a view that the restriction was probably not intended to
> cover the situation. Submit the agenda as it stands and accept the determination.

## 5. Statements made by attendees

The **Research Analyst** confirmed no publication, no rating change and no target price change since the
restriction, that two client calls on the name had been declined with a note that no comment
could be offered, and that they would not attend the 8 October meeting or accept a request to
write new material for a client meeting while the name is restricted.

The **Markets Lead** confirmed that no derivative structure had been solicited and that none
would be until the Control Room ruled on whether the proposed hedging overlay touches the
restricted subject matter.

The **Credit Risk Lead** confirmed that the pro-forma covenant test in preparation is built entirely
on filed financial statements and the Bank's own facility documentation, with no non-public
content.

**Coverage** confirmed that no hold size, price or structure had been indicated to the client
as firm, and that the indication given on 15 September was expressly qualified as subject to
internal approval and recorded as such in the call report.
