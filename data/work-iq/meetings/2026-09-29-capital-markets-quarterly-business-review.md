## 1. Book performance — FY26 year to date

The Chair reviewed the ten-name Energy and adjacent coverage book. Aggregate FY26
year-to-date revenue is ahead of the same period in FY25, driven disproportionately by two
names. The Chair cautioned against reading book-level growth as broad-based.

**Concentration of revenue.** Gibson Energy alone contributes **CAD 8.42m** of FY26
year-to-date revenue against **CAD 7.14m** in the same period of FY25 — up **17.9 per cent**
and the largest single contributor in the book. Wallet share on the name is estimated at
**21.4 per cent**, up from 18.9, moving the Bank from third to **second**.

The Chair's observation, minuted at their request: *"A book whose growth depends on one name
is not a growing book. It is a concentrated one. I want the second half to prove otherwise."*

**Product mix.** Across the book, Debt Capital Markets and Loan Syndications carry the
revenue. Equity Capital Markets is materially under-represented. On Gibson specifically the
Bank has **zero** ECM revenue against an estimated **CAD 2.8m** of street wallet, and
**CAD 0.45m** of M&A against an estimated **CAD 4.5m** — the Bank financed the most recent
acquisition without advising on it.

The Research Analyst (item 1 only) noted that sector-wide the announce–bridge–term-out–deleverage
sequence has been the dominant pattern for eighteen months and that the advisory fee pool
has concentrated in a small number of houses. They left the meeting at the end of this item.

## 2. Credit and capacity constraints on the book

Credit Risk presented the sector position: **Energy at CAD 2,744m against a CAD 3,200m
ceiling — 85.8 per cent, AMBER** under CP-002, with a hard Midstream and Infrastructure
sub-ceiling of CAD 1,600m.

**Agreed consequence for origination planning.** For the remainder of FY26, new lead-arranged
Energy transactions are planned on an originate-to-distribute basis with the distribution
plan documented before commitment. Two names in the book are watchlisted on deteriorating
trend and are closed to incremental exposure.

Credit Risk also noted that across the book, **derivative pre-settlement lines are sub-limits
of single-name credit limits and consume them**. Where Corporate Sales and Corporate Banking
are both growing on the same name, they are competing for one limit, and origination plans
that assume otherwise will not clear committee.

## 4. Conduct and control items

Coverage reported **one overdue open commitment** in the book — the cash management
comparison owed to Gibson Energy since 16 July 2026, then eleven weeks outstanding. The Chair
recorded that repeatedly carrying an unclosed commitment forward is a relationship management
failure reportable in the quarterly conduct review, and required it closed before 8 October.

Coverage flagged the periodic **KYC refresh due 25 November 2026** on Gibson Energy with two
documents outstanding, and the CP-006 consequence that from the due date no committed
facility increase may be completed. The Chair required that KYC due dates be surfaced at the
point a transaction is contemplated across the whole book, not at documentation.
