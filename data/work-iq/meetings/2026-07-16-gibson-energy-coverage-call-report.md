## Matters discussed

**Post-acquisition integration.** The Treasurer walked through the first full quarter of the
acquired infrastructure assets. Their framing was that the assets are performing but the
balance sheet has not caught up — the facility was sized for a smaller company and the
capital structure is being managed quarter to quarter rather than strategically.

**September note offering.** Confirmed the Bank's role as an active bookrunner on the offering then in
preparation. The Treasurer was complimentary about execution and specifically about the
distribution outcome. This is the strongest position the relationship has been in.

**Cash management.** The Director, Capital Markets raised — unprompted — that they are
reviewing their transaction banking arrangements post-acquisition, primarily because the
cross-border USD flows have changed shape. They asked for a comparison of our proposition
against their incumbent.

**Rating.** They restated that maintaining investment grade is a board-level constraint and
that anything which pressures it will not be entertained regardless of economics.
