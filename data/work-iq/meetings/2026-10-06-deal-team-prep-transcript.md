**Coverage Lead:** Two days out. I want to walk the agenda, and I want each of you to tell me the thing
you are least comfortable with rather than the thing you are most ready to present. Corporate Banking Lead,
please start.

**Corporate Banking Lead:** Item one is the relationship review and it is the easiest thing we do all day. FY26
year to date CAD 8.42m against CAD 7.14m in the same period last year, up 17.9 per cent.
Wallet share 21.4, up from 18.9. Second from third. Number one in commodity derivatives.

**Coverage Lead:** None of which we show them.

**Corporate Banking Lead:** None of which we show them. CP-014 — wallet share estimates and competitor rankings
do not leave the building. What I show them is what we did: bookrunner on the September offering,
the bridge take-out, the hedging programme. The facts, not our scoring of them.

**Coverage Lead:** Good. What are you least comfortable with?

**Corporate Banking Lead:** Item two. They are going to ask for a number and I do not have one to give them.
CAD 210m needs a convened Credit Committee under CP-003, five business days, no expedited
path. And the sector is amber so it also needs Portfolio Management and a documented
distribution plan under CP-002.

**Coverage Lead:** Then I take that question, not you. If the Treasurer presses, I say we are working it
through our approvals and we will come back with something firm. I am not having you be the
one holding a line you did not set.

**Credit Risk Lead:** Can I add to that. The single-name picture is not the problem. CAD 341.7m of CAD 500m
is 68.3 per cent, green, plenty of room. The binding constraint is the sector — CAD 2,744m of
CAD 3,200m, 85.8 per cent, amber. An incremental CAD 45m of hold is nothing to them and it is
not nothing to us.

**Coverage Lead:** And we do not tell them that.

**Credit Risk Lead:** We absolutely do not tell them that. CP-002 prohibits using our capacity as a
negotiating position and it prohibits disclosing it. If we are constrained, the line is that
the hold is subject to internal approval, full stop.

**Coverage Lead:** Credit Risk Lead, the covenant. Say it the way you would say it to the CFO if you were allowed to.

**Credit Risk Lead:** Total Net Debt to Adjusted EBITDA is 4.18x against a 4.50x maximum. That is 0.32
turns. Under CP-005 that is the Tight band — below 0.35. Last quarter it was 3.94x. The move
is acquisition debt service, not operating deterioration, which is why we held the rating at
IG-6 rather than downgrading.

**Coverage Lead:** And the pro-forma.

**Credit Risk Lead:** Done and signed off, fully drawn basis at the next two test dates, as CP-005 requires
before the pitch is delivered — before pitch, not before mandate. At 1.35 fully drawn it is
4.59x. That breaches. The most that clears is 195.8 of new commitment, and that is at zero
headroom.

**Coverage Lead:** So your answer is no.

**Credit Risk Lead:** My answer is not at 1.35. A smaller facility with a covenant conversation attached
is a different question. Negotiate relief or a step-down at the same time as the upsize, not as a follow-on request in six months when the
headroom has gone. Asking now, while the relationship is strong and they need something from
us, is a much better conversation than asking after a bad print.

**Corporate Banking Lead:** That is the strategic point of the whole meeting, actually. Not the size.

**Coverage Lead:** Agreed. Markets Lead, over to you.

**Markets Lead:** Item three, and I am going to be blunt. I have Option A built — split notional,
smaller differential piece, larger CAD/USD strip, CAD 240m total over 24 months, fits inside
the pre-settlement line, three-scenario payoff illustration drafted per CP-010 Tier 2
including the adverse case where the differential goes against them while the outright goes
their way.

**Coverage Lead:** That is the scenario they never expect.

**Markets Lead:** It is the one that generates the complaint eighteen months later, yes. But I have two
problems. One, I still do not have the documented contracted volume split. CP-010 says the
underlying exposure is documented before the structure is proposed. Without it I present a
framework and questions, not a notional.

**Coverage Lead:** And two?

**Markets Lead:** No Control Room clearance against CR-2026-0184. And I am not going to take a view on
whether a structure sized to the acquired volumes touches the restricted matter. That is
their call, not mine. If it does not come by Wednesday, drop item three.

**Coverage Lead:** That is the right answer and I want it minuted. Nobody in the Bank gets criticised
for standing down when the answer is unclear.

**Credit Risk Lead:** One thing on the limit that neither of you has connected. The pre-settlement line is
a sub-limit of the single-name limit and it consumes it under CP-004. So a bigger derivative
position and a bigger loan hold are competing for the same CAD 500m. We are not choosing
between two products. We are choosing how to spend one limit.

**Coverage Lead:** That is the sentence I want in the brief.

**Corporate Banking Lead:** Item four, the 2027 refinancing — I am flagging that we are pitching for a role in a
future financing while a restriction is live. It may be fine. It may not be. Same answer:
Control Room decides.

**Coverage Lead:** Same answer. Item five?

**Corporate Banking Lead:** Sustainability-linked. No framework, no second-party opinion. Under CP-011 we can
have an indicative conversation and we cannot label anything. I will keep it at 'what would
need to be true' — material KPIs, an ambition test the acquisition does not accidentally
satisfy, symmetrical pricing, second-party opinion before launch.

**Coverage Lead:** Do not let it drift into marketing. Housekeeping.

**Corporate Banking Lead:** KYC refresh due 25 November, 51 days, two documents outstanding. Under CP-006 from
that date there is no grace period — no new products, no committed facility increase
completed, no new derivative documentation executed. If Credit Committee lands late
November and the refresh is not done, we get an approval we cannot execute.

**Credit Risk Lead:** That is the real deadline on this transaction. Not the committee calendar.

**Coverage Lead:** Raise it as housekeeping. Not as a condition. It must not read as leverage.

**Corporate Banking Lead:** Agreed. And sanctions — clean screen 27 September on the obligor and beneficial owners,
but the terminal and marine counterparties on the acquired volumes were not in that run.
CP-007 is explicit that a clean screen on the obligor is not a clean screen on the flow. It
goes in the brief as a stated gap rather than being left out.

**Coverage Lead:** Right. Last thing. The cash management comparison we owed them from 16 July.

**Corporate Banking Lead:** Closed Sunday. Eleven weeks late. It is in the brief as an overdue open commitment
and it is in the call report as my failure.

**Coverage Lead:** Thank you for putting it in rather than hoping I would not look. Closing thought.
This relationship went from third to second because we showed up with balance sheet when
they needed it. The risk now is reading that as permission to ask for everything at once.
Five items, a live restriction, a tight covenant, an amber sector and a KYC clock. Priority
order if we lose time: relationship review and revolver first, because that is what they
asked us for. Differential hedge second, because that is the problem they told us they have.
Everything else optional.

If anyone is uncomfortable in the room, about anything, say you will come back rather than
answer. Coming back is free.
