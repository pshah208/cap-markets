## 1. Sector position

The Chair opened with the aggregate position. Energy sector exposure stands at
**CAD 2,744m against a CAD 3,200m ceiling — 85.8 per cent, AMBER** under CP-002.
The Midstream and Infrastructure sub-ceiling of CAD 1,600m was noted as hard; unused
headroom in Exploration & Production does not create capacity in Midstream.

**Consequence agreed.** Every new commitment in the sector, regardless of the obligor's own
single-name headroom, requires Portfolio Management sign-off plus an identified offset —
a syndication, a sell-down, a maturing facility or an approved distribution plan. The
default posture for new lead-arranged transactions is arrangement position above final
hold, with the distribution plan documented **before** commitment.

The Chair noted that the correlated cluster of obligors dependent on the same egress
corridor is the relevant exposure for stress purposes, and that adding to the largest
member of that cluster is a concentration decision as much as a client decision.

| Public ratings | S&P BBB- (stable) / DBRS BBB (low) (stable) |
| Last full credit review | 18 March 2026 · next due 31 March 2027 |

**Covenant position.** The tightest covenant is Total Net Debt to Adjusted EBITDA at
**4.18x against a 4.50x maximum — 0.32 turns of headroom**, which places the obligor in the
**Tight band** under CP-005. The prior quarter was 3.94x; the move is attributable to a full
quarter of acquisition-related debt service rather than to operating deterioration.

Senior secured leverage 2.61x of 3.50x, interest coverage 4.12x against a 2.50x minimum, and
tangible net worth CAD 1,486m against a CAD 1,150m minimum are all comfortable.

**Rating decision.** The rating was held at IG-6 at the 31 July review. Two quarters ago the
obligor was IG-7. The Chair recorded the rationale for holding rather than downgrading:
the leverage increase is acquisition-funded and the contracted cash flow mix improved after
close. He also recorded the rationale for **not** watchlisting despite the Tight covenant
band, as CP-005 requires that rationale to be written down.

**Add-back discipline.** The borrower's covenant calculation includes run-rate acquisition
synergies. The Chair confirmed the unadjusted ratio is tracked in parallel. Adjusted and
unadjusted do not straddle a headroom band this quarter but will if realised synergies lag
the pro-forma case.

## 3. Proposed revolver upsize — discussion

Coverage described the client's indication of a move from CAD 1.10bn to approximately
CAD 1.35bn with a one-year extension, and a BMO hold moving from CAD 165m to CAD 210m.

The Chair set out the approvals that would be required and asked that they be listed in any
pitch preparation rather than discovered later:

1. **Convened Credit Committee** under CP-003 — CAD 210m falls in the CAD 151m–250m band.
   Five business days, no expedited path.
2. **Portfolio Management sign-off plus a documented distribution plan** under CP-002,
   because the sector is amber.
3. **Pro-forma covenant test** under CP-005, on a fully drawn assumption at the next two
   scheduled test dates, completed and signed off **before the pitch is delivered** — not
   before mandate — because the transaction would complete within two quarters of a test date.

**Action.** Chair to produce the pro-forma by 6 August 2026. Coverage not to indicate a hold
size before it is complete. *(Agreed unanimously.)*
